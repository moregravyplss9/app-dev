## My Favorite Series: *Stranger Things*

> "Friends don't lie."
> — Eleven

**Stranger Things** is a sci-fi horror series set in the small town of **Hawkins, Indiana** in the 1980s. It follows a group of friends who face monsters, secret experiments, and a dark world called the **Upside Down**. It has **5 seasons** in total and is streaming on **Netflix**.

## All Seasons

### Season 1: The Vanishing of Will Byers

Will Byers disappears, and his friends Mike, Dustin, and Lucas meet a mysterious girl named **Eleven**. Together they search for Will and face the **Demogorgon**.

### Season 2: The Mind Flayer

Will is still affected by the Upside Down, and a new threat called the **Mind Flayer** begins to spread over Hawkins.

### Season 3: The Starcourt Mall

During the summer of 1985, the new **Starcourt Mall** hides a dangerous secret, and the Mind Flayer returns in a new form.

### Season 4: Vecna

The group is split across different places, and a new villain named **Vecna** starts attacking teenagers in Hawkins.

### Season 5: The Final Season

The story comes to its final chapter as the friends make their last stand against the threat that has haunted Hawkins.

### Why I Like It

1. The **story** keeps me hooked from season to season.
2. The **characters** grow and feel real.
3. The *soundtrack* and 80s setting are great.

### Favorite Characters

- Eleven
  - Why I like her: Why I like her: She is strong and brave even though she went through so much in the lab. Her friendship with Mike and the group shows how much she cares for the people she loves.

- Dustin
  - Why I like him: Why I like him: He is funny and smart, and he always brings humor to the group even in scary moments. He is also a loyal friend who never gives up on the people around him.
- Steve
  - Why I like him: Why I like him: He starts as a typical popular kid but grows into a caring "babysitter" for the kids. His protective and selfless attitude makes him one of the best characters in the show.
